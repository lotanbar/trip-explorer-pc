# Privacy

Trip Explorer (the PC app and the phone app) is a personal app with one user: its author.

- The apps sign in with Google only to sync the user's own `trips/` folder with a folder the user picks on their own
  Google Drive. Files go directly between the device and Google Drive.
- No data is sent to the author or to any other server. There are no analytics and no ads.
- The Google sign-in token is stored only on the device, in the app's own storage. Signing out, or removing the app's
  access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, stops all access.
